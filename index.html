Common questions

How long does delivery take?

Usually 2-3 days. The laptop is ordered from the wholesaler for you, so it can sometimes take longer.

How does payment work?

Confirm your order with a 50% advance and pay the remaining 50% when you receive the laptop. Payment details are shared on WhatsApp.

How long is the price valid?

Prices on the website are valid for 48 hours. The final rate is confirmed on WhatsApp.

Can I see the laptop before I pay?

Yes. After you choose a laptop, we send pictures of your exact laptop on WhatsApp. You confirm it, then pay the advance.
